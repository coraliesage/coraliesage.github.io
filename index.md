Hi! I'm Coralie, currently a MSc student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich, focusing on Machine Intelligence with a minor in Data Management. 
I'm interested in researching the intersection between AI safety, alignment and social sciences, specifically with respect to public policy, democracy and governance. 

During my studies, I've been fortunate to work with the Center for Law and Economics at ETH Zürich under Prof. Stefan Bechtold and at the DISCO Group under Prof. Roger Wattenhofer for my undergraduate thesis. 

Alongside my studies, I've pursued various personal interests, including working as a Deeptech Venture Capital Analyst, competing in and organising cybersecurity public policy competitions, volunteering as an auditor and legal board member for student organisations, and mentoring junior and high schoolers in Maths and Programming.
